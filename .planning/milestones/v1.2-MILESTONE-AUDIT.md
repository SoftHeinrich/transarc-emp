## Honesty note (carried into the paper)

The headline story is **not** "macro beats micro." Once the universe is reconciled the
per-system micro/macro *gap* is small (aggregation is second-order); macro's only edge is
a modest separation margin (0.620 vs 0.528, +0.092), and the **first-order discriminator
is worst-component tail coverage** (`min_comp`). The Ch2 prose states this exactly,
matching `reports/METRIC_FITNESS.md`.
